## 2. LYRA screening result (July 2026, web-level)

Material conflicts in or adjacent to our Nice classes (9, 35, 36, 42):

- **Lyra Network** — French payment service provider / e-payment gateway
  (Payzen), operating since 2001, international reach. *Direct fintech conflict.*
- **Lyra Health** — US health-benefits platform, registered LYRA trademark.
  *Health/insurance-adjacent conflict.*
- **Lyra Client Solutions** — fintech client-servicing venture (Motive Partners
  + Apollo, launched 2025); insurance is within the sponsor's stated subsectors.
  *Recent, well-capitalised, financial services.*
- **LYRA (Reg. #6442695)** — registered in computer & software services class
  (media streaming use). *Class 42 overlap.*
- **Lyra Software** (MX) — business applications. *Software conflict.*

Assessment: LYRA is a common astronomical/mythological term with existing
registered use in software and active commercial use in payments and health
benefits. Coexistence may be arguable on goods/services distinctions, but this
is materially higher risk than QIVANTA and a UAE/GCC registry search is likely
to surface further hits. This is a web-level screen, not legal clearance.

## 3. Options

| Option | Description | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Proceed as LYRA** | Use as instructed; counsel-led clearance in target classes/markets; accept coexistence risk and possible narrowing | Fastest to the story; real risk of opposition or forced rebrand |
| **B — LYRAE** | File the star-nomenclature genitive (as in *Alpha Lyrae*); keeps the entire brand story, constellation, and Vega mark | One letter of distinctiveness; needs its own screen but likely cleaner |
| **C — QIVANTA house / Lyra internal** | Keep the already-screened QIVANTA as the registrable house mark; use "Lyra" as the internal platform codename and never publicly | Zero brand risk; loses the public poetry of Lyra |
| **D — Composite** | "LYRA by goNXT" or "goNXT Lyra" | Composites rarely cure class conflicts on the dominant element |

**Recommendation:** build as LYRA (this pack), screen **LYRAE** in parallel, and
keep QIVANTA reserved. Decide before the first public-facing deployment or
marketing surface.

## 4. Why the cost of being wrong is low here

`brand.houseMark` is a configuration token (docs/01 §6, CLAUDE.md rule 5). No
user-facing string, email template, PDF header, mobile app name or deployed
domain hard-codes the house mark. A rename is a config change plus a domain
change — hours, not a project. This was designed in deliberately.
